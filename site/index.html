Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Bonsai is open source under the MIT license, with no trial, no account and no watermark.

Will my PDF still open everywhere?

Images are saved as baseline JPEG, the form every PDF reader decodes, and every page is compared with the original before the file is saved. Results were checked in Preview, Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Mail and Telegram on iPhone, and the engines of Android and Linux viewers.

What happens to my original?

By default Bonsai saves a copy next to it. With Replace original, the original goes to the Trash only after the new file is safely written.

Why does it need Homebrew?

The heavy lifting is done by qpdf and mozjpeg, two proven open source tools. Bonsai runs them as separate programs, so they update on their own and the app stays small.

Which files shrink the most?

Decks, portfolios and reports exported from Figma, Keynote, PowerPoint, Canva or InDesign, where large photos sit behind small frames. Scans that are already low resolution shrink the least.
